What Core automates

Rules written for D&D 2024

Any spell, feature or monster ability is played from dnd5e's own data: attack roll, saving throw, damage, healing, effects. That is why Fireball, Bless or Cure Wounds are not listed here: they need nothing more. The lists below are what the data alone can't do, and what Core adds on top: 448 rules keyed to dnd5e identifiers, so they apply to any creature, player character or monster.

DAS is in beta. Not every spell and feature is covered yet: some still need a click or a roll by hand.

Built into the engine

Coded into the engine itself, for every creature, rather than tied to one item.

  • Weapon Mastery properties
  • Opportunity attacks, before the move
  • Cover and line of sight
  • Grappling and shoving
  • Hiding, Search and passive Perception
  • Help (attack and ability checks)
  • Concentration
  • Death saving throws and stabilizing
  • Prone, crawling and standing up
  • Wild Shape
  • Metamagic
  • Bardic Inspiration and other aid dice
  • Superiority dice (Battle Master)
  • Smites
  • Summons and familiars
  • Potions and spell scrolls
  • Carried light sources (torches, lamps, lanterns)
  • Legendary actions and resistances
  • Doors and locks

Something missing or wrong? Tell us on Discord or open an issue.
