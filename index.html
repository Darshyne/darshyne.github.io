Required

DAS · Core

The rules and combat engine. It owns the whole combat loop, so nothing fights over your clicks.

  • Click the ground to move along a path, click an enemy to attack
  • Opportunity attacks resolved before the move
  • Reactions and saving throws on each player's own screen
  • Lingering zones, concentration, conditions, auras
  • Vision and light rules: who sees whom, Search, passive Perception

Needs Foundry V14 and dnd5e 6.0 or later. Patches neither Foundry nor the system.

Needs Core

DAS · Combat UI

The screen you play from: an action bar along the bottom, party portraits on the side, the turn order across the top.

  • Actions grouped by tab, layout saved per character
  • Pick a spell's level from a drawer
  • Greyed-out actions tell you why
  • Movement budget, Dash and the path shown before you commit
Needs Core

DAS · Loot & Trade

What happens after the fight: corpses to search, chests to pick, merchants to haggle with.

  • Treasure rolled on the first search, by challenge rating
  • Locked containers: key, thieves' tools or the GM
  • Pickpocketing, items thrown on the ground, raising the dead
  • Barter window with restocking merchants
Standalone

DAS · Shadows & Ambience

Tokens that sit in the scene. Purely visual: it never writes anything to your world.

  • Contact shadows, and shadows cast by lights and the sun
  • Flying creatures bob; higher tokens look larger
  • Blur beyond darkvision range
Needs BLFX Premium

DAS · Animations

Boss Loot animations for the abilities Boss Loot does not recognise on its own, such as high-level spells. Your own custom animations are kept.

Boss Loot is a paid third-party module, not included.

Needs Core

DAS · Homebrew

Make your own automated spells, features and magic items step by step, without writing code.

Install

Paste a link into Foundry

DAS is not listed in Foundry's package browser. Install it from these links: updates then arrive through Foundry's usual Check for Updates. The same links work on The Forge.

1

Install Core first

In Foundry, go to Add-on Modules → Install Module, paste the link at the bottom, then Install.

Good to know

  • The interface is in English and French. Other languages can be added: each module has a translation file.

  • Back up your world before trying DAS on a campaign in progress.
